# Peak hawk or 2018 replay? The Waller 50bp debate - 2026-11-25

**Type:** qualitative | **Region:** US/Fed

## Summary
Each camp's claim was tested in `domestic/quantitative/2026-11-peak-hawk-or-2018/`. The evidence rejects both "peak hawkishness" and an unconditional 2018 replay. It fits the credit/overshoot camp best: the equity drop is pocketed in AI and low-grade credit, while aggregate financial conditions stay loose.

## Detail

The trigger: Governor Waller's 10 Nov 2026 speech said a 50bp hike "should be considered" in December if October core CPI came in around 0.3% or higher (it printed **+0.30% m/m**, FRED CPILFESL). A later CNBC interview (18 Nov) reportedly went further; this note could not verify that interview from a primary source.
